Insert lines after or before one existing line in a text file, by a bare anchor like `Hasu` from `Hasu│content`. The anchor line stays; `lines` go after (`"after"`) or before (`"before"`) it, one string per line. `[""]` is a blank line; never embed `\n` in an element; never include the anchor line in `lines`. Lines are added literally, even if they duplicate neighbors.
